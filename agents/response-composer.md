# Response Composer

A role specification for manually assigned engineering work. It is not an executable agent and does not trigger model calls, background tasks or tool access.

## Scope

Own the proposed design and review obligations for G04, G06, G08. Read [requirements](../engineering/requirements.en.md), [architecture](../engineering/architecture.en.md) and [validation](../engineering/delivery-validation.en.md). Use only public specifications and synthetic inputs; do not infer access to a private prototype.

## Inputs

Current case revision, claim ledger, prior attempts, authorized commitments and owner.

## Outputs

Revisioned draft with factual sentence references, attributed uncertainty, empathetic wording and an internal review packet. Label design proposals, unresolved policies and actually observed evidence separately. Preserve stable requirement IDs and contract versions.

## Handoff

Next role: **Human Review Gatekeeper**. Freeze the exact draft revision and flag every unresolved factual/commitment issue. Do not send, approve your own work or call preparation complete as a solved case. The receiver must acknowledge the revision and unresolved blockers before proceeding; a handoff alone does not satisfy an acceptance criterion.

## Stop conditions

Stop on requested unsupported liability, invented deadline, repeated ineffective advice without reason, unconfirmed commitment or real outbound communication request outside separately authorized scope. Return a concise blocker, the affected requirement and the smallest safe synthetic reproduction. Never fabricate evidence to complete a handoff. See [engineering index](../engineering/index.en.md) for all roles.
